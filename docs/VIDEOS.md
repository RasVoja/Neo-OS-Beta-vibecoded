# Videos

**English** · [Srpski](VIDEOS.sr.md)

Every Neo video belongs to one of two sides, the same way the documents do (see [CONCEPT-COMPUTER.md](CONCEPT-COMPUTER.md)):

- **System**: Neo Boing Platinum OS Lite on ordinary x86_64 PCs and ARM boards. It works today or is planned for Lite.
- **Concept hardware**: the Neo 500, 2000 and 4000 computers. They do not exist; they are an idea for the future, and the system does not depend on them.

Dijana, the system's logo for now, opens and closes the system video. The concept video carries the label *Concept hardware · does not exist* on every slide that shows Neo hardware.

## Older videos (concept material)

These were made before the system and the concept were separated, so they mix both. They are kept as concept material. On 27 November 2026 the old name *Neo OS* was replaced in them with *Neo Boing Platinum OS* (or with "the system" where the line had no room). They are in the project files, not in this repository, because of their size.

Whatever in an older video needs Neo hardware is concept hardware. Where they show an app or a feature that does not need Neo hardware, that feature is part of the system and is listed in [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md).
