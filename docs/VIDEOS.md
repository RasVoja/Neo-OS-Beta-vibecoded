# Videos

**English** · [Srpski](VIDEOS.sr.md)

Every Neo video belongs to one of two sides, the same way the documents do (see [CONCEPT-COMPUTER.md](CONCEPT-COMPUTER.md)):

- **System**: Neo Boing Platinum OS Lite on ordinary x86_64 PCs and ARM boards. It works today or is planned for Lite.
- **Concept hardware**: the Neo 500, 2000 and 4000 computers. They do not exist; they are an idea for the future, and the system does not depend on them.

Dijana, the system's logo for now, opens and closes the system videos and sits in the corner of the feature videos. The concept video carries the label *Concept hardware · does not exist* on every slide that shows Neo hardware.

## Feature videos (concept material)

These show the features and ideas one by one: apps, everyday life, Dijana's personality, the Crystal style with the Snake. They were made before the system and the concept were separated, so they mix both. On 27 November 2026 the old name *Neo OS* in them was replaced with *Neo Boing Platinum OS* (or "the system" where the line had no room). On 28 November 2026 they were rendered again with a label *Concept · not built yet* in the top corner of every frame and Dijana in the bottom corner, and 720p copies were added here.
