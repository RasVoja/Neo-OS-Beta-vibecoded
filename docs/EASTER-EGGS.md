# Easter eggs, slogans and lines for the day

**English** · [Srpski](EASTER-EGGS.sr.md) · 28 November 2026

The system has a sense of humour, but it never gets in the way. Every joke here is small, can be switched off in one place (*Settings → Personalization → Little jokes*), never animates on battery and respects "reduce motion". Each item says whether it is **in the ISO today** (Neo Boing Platinum OS Lite, real code that CI boots) or **planned**.

## The platinum ball (Skočica)

A nod to the bouncing ball of the old Amiga demos, drawn our own way: a **platinum sphere** with fine engraved lines, a soft reflection and a small red star from Dijana's scarf. It is *not* the red and white checkered Boing Ball: that logo is a registered trademark of its owner, and the project never uses it (see the note in the [README](../README.md)). *Boing* in the name is a word-nod only.

| Where | What | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neo DOS | `BOING` prints the ball bouncing across the line and "Every idea has its place." | **in the ISO today** |
| HTML demos | click Dijana in the page header **5 times**: the ball drops in, bounces three times with a squash and a shadow, and rolls out | **in the demos today** |
| Boot splash | the ball falls onto the platinum piece (below) and bounces once while the system starts; one frame on a slow machine | planned |
| Idle | after 20 minutes with no input, at most once an hour, the ball rolls along the Bar; Dijana's eyes follow it, she sometimes chases it | planned |
| Videos | the next feature video opens and closes with the ball (see [VIDEOS.md](VIDEOS.md)) | planned |

## The platinum piece

The visual motif behind the name *Platinum*: a piece of polished platinum, silver-white, with a soft cold light moving across it (a nod to the Platinum look of Mac OS 8 and 9, in our own drawing).

- **Splash screen background**: the piece lies at the centre, the light moves once across it, then Dijana and the name *Neo Boing Platinum OS* fade in. *Planned.*
- **Animated intro** for videos and the first start (2 to 3 seconds, can be skipped with any key). *Planned.*
- Colours for the visual identity: platinum `#E5E4E2`, light silver `#F4F4F2`, shadow `#8A8D91`, plus Dijana's red `#C8102E` for the star. The theme *Neo Platinum* uses them.

## The bomb and the sad face

The classic Mac showed a bomb, the old error screens a sad face. Ours, drawn by us: on a serious error Dijana looks sad, and in the *Retro* theme a small round **bomb with a lit fuse** sits next to the NEO error code. Clicking the bomb shows "Not a real bomb. Your files are safe." *Planned* (the sad Dijana is in the drawing list in [MASCOT.md](MASCOT.md)).

## Plan 9 and outer space

Neo Boing Platinum OS is **inspired by Plan 9 from Bell Labs**, on purpose: everything is a file, every program sees its own namespace, the network is just another place (see [NETWORK-DATA.md](NETWORK-DATA.md)). And with a wink at **Plan 9 from Outer Space** (1957), the most loved "worst film ever".

| Where | What | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neo DOS | `PLAN9` draws a flying saucer in ASCII and says "Greetings, my friend. We are all interested in the future." | **in the ISO today** |
| Kernel panic | after the technical lines, one more: "Don't panic: something went wrong with the universe." (Plan 9 meets *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*). The real reason always comes first. The kernel's own font is ASCII only, so the Serbian line, "Ne paniči: nešto je pošlo naopako sa svemirom.", comes with the desk's error screen | **in the ISO today** |
| Panic screen (desk) | the saucer flies slowly across the top of the error screen, Dijana watches it | planned |

## The War of the Worlds

H. G. Wells, 1898 (and the famous radio play of 1938).

- Neo DOS: `ULLA` (the cry of the Martians in the novel) draws a tripod and says "No panic: it is only a radio play. Dijana is guarding the disk." **In the ISO today.**
- Planned: when a backup finishes, now and then: "The Martians are coming? Fine, we have a backup."

## Stephen King and the Bin

- When you restore a file from the Bin, now and then: **"Sometimes they come back."** *Planned.*
- A red balloon floats out of an emptied Bin once a year, on 31 October, with "We all float down here." Switched off for children's accounts. *Planned.*

## Lines for the day (KOAN)

`KOAN` (or `FORTUNE`) in Neo DOS prints one line, picked by the clock. **In the ISO today.** On the desk the same line will show in Dijana's bubble at start-up, or on her tile. The built-in list:

| Line | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Every idea has its place. | the system's motto |
| An OS by people, for people. | the system |
| We are not from outer space, but our system is. | a Plan 9 nod |
| We all float down here. | Stephen King, *It* |
| Fear is the mind-killer. | Frank Herbert, *Dune* |
| Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic. | Arthur C. Clarke |
| My God, it's full of stars! | Arthur C. Clarke, *2001: A Space Odyssey* |
| It's a dangerous business, going out your door. | J. R. R. Tolkien, *The Lord of the Rings* |
| The truth is a matter of the imagination. | Ursula K. Le Guin, *The Left Hand of Darkness* |
| Ulla! | H. G. Wells, *The War of the Worlds* |
| Don't panic. | Douglas Adams |
| Greetings, my friend. We are all interested in the future. | *Plan 9 from Outer Space* |
| The past underneath, the future in view. | the system |

Only short lines, always with the author and the work; nothing longer is shipped. Two lines that are often quoted wrongly are left out: "The only way to deal with an unfree world..." is Albert Camus, not Le Guin, and "Be without fear in the face of your enemies" is from the film *Kingdom of Heaven*. "The spice must flow" is from the 1984 film, so the list uses Herbert's own line from the book.

## A name for your computer

At the first start the system asks **"What is your computer called?"**, and offers a few names to pick or to change: *Rohan*, *Deep Thought*, *HAL 9001* (one better than 9000), *Marvin*, *Eddie*, *Stardust*, *Fortress*, *Arrakis*, *Earthsea*. Dijana then greets by name: "Welcome to Rohan." The name is also the computer's name on the home network and in Neo DOS's prompt. *Planned.* Today: `ECHO Welcome to Rohan.` in `DH0:s/user-startup`.

## The system speaks in haiku (HAIKU)

Now and then the system says something as a real haiku, 5, 7 and 5 syllables. `HAIKU` in Neo DOS prints one, in Serbian or English. **In the ISO today.** On the desk (planned) a haiku can replace the plain message at start-up, after a small error, or when the computer has been idle a long time, at most once a day, never in place of what you need to know.

| When | English | Srpski |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start-up | Quiet code awakes, / pixels reaching for the light, / the screen yawns at dawn. | Kod se budi tih, / pikseli traže svetlost, / ekran zine dan. |
| Error | Something is not here, / bytes wander in the darkness, / Dijana keeps watch. | Nešto nije tu, / bajtovi lutaju tmom, / Dijana čuva. |
| Idle | The mouse waits in peace, / thoughts are gathering somewhere, / press Enter, my friend. | Miš čeka miran, / misli se skupljaju tu, / pritisni Enter. |

## Silly mode

In the spirit of the *silly walk* option in some comedy games: a mode where the system plays the fool, for a laugh, never for work. On with *Settings → Fun → Silly mode*, or the secret: type `SILLY` in Neo DOS, or press the Neo key and S, I, L, L, Y on the desk. Off with the same, and by itself at midnight. *Planned* (only `KNIGHT` is in the ISO today).

- Dijana walks across the screen in a ridiculous walk, high steps and all.
- Windows open with a short fanfare (a CC0 sound from the sound pack).
- The Bin is called **the Holy Hand Grenade** and asks: "Are you sure? Are you REALLY sure? Three shall be the number thou shalt count..." One click still empties it.
- Renaming a file suggests: "Call it Duck?"
- Every menu gets one extra item, *Something completely different*, which shows a random haiku.
- **A computer with a personality**, in the spirit of a certain cheeky robot from *Futurama*: "Permission denied" gets "Bite my shiny metal... no." and a warning says "I am 40% sure this will work." (Grown-up accounts only.)
- **Vogon poetry** (*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*) for a timeout: a short, deliberately awful verse of our own, for example "Oh freddled packet, / thou hast waited much too long, / like a gruntbuggly modem."
- **Only Fools and Horses** after a crash: the real message first (what stopped, and what to do), then one line under it: "He who dares, Rodney..." or "You plonker!"
- **Life of Brian** on long progress bars: "Always look on the bright side of life" scrolls under the bar, one word every few seconds.

## Your own motto

- **Today**: put an `ECHO` line in `DH0:s/user-startup`, for example `ECHO Every day a little better.`, and Neo DOS shows it every time it starts.
- **Planned**: *Settings → Personalization → My motto*. What you type there shows at start-up in Dijana's bubble, or on her tile. Empty means a random line from the list above. You can add your own lines, and pick the **Haiku** style: the field counts syllables (5, 7, 5) in Serbian and English and shows the poem on three lines. Lines never come from the network, so there is no push and no advertising.

## Rules for every joke

1. It never hides the real message: an error first says what happened and what to do.
2. One switch turns them all off; children's accounts get only the friendly ones.
3. Never on battery, never with "reduce motion", never during a presentation or a full-screen game.
4. Our own drawings and short attributed quotes only; no trademarked logos.
