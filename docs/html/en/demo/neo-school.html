Neo School: one lesson, three ages

The same lesson works for a seven-year-old, a pupil and an engineer. Only how much the system shows changes: pictures and buttons, then numbers and a formula, then a table, air resistance and data export. The teacher sees progress in the Classroom with no cloud at all.

NEOSchool
Change the age. A child gets big buttons and stars, a pupil gets the formula and a question, a pro gets Moon and Mars gravity, air resistance and a CSV for the spreadsheet.
Try the Turtle. A child stacks blocks, a pupil writes Neo BASIC in English or Serbian (FORWARD or its Serbian word), a pro sees the same program translated to Python.
Open the Classroom. The teacher sends the lesson to the whole class and chooses how much Claude may help. Marks and progress stay on the school computer.

Neo is a concept. This is a simulation in the browser, not the real system.
