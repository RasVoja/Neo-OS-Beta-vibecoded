English done properly, and any other language

NeoOS speaks English in British and American flavours, down to spelling, dates, paper and the name of the Bin. For any other official language there is a localisation kit: a table, checks and a package for the Treasury, with no programming.

One system, four languages

Switch the language and watch what changes: menus, plurals, dates, money, temperature, paper, keyboard, spelling and the order things sort in.

Sorting words

British and American spelling, in one go

Type in British English on the left and the American version appears on the right, or the other way round. The spell checker and the dictionary use the same rules.

Words whose meaning differs between the two (licence and license, for example) are flagged for the writer rather than changed.

Localisation kit: a new language in an afternoon

A translator fills in the table and the kit checks it at once: what is missing, how many plural forms the language needs, whether a label is too long for its button, and whether the language is written right to left. When everything is green, the package goes to the Treasury like any other.
