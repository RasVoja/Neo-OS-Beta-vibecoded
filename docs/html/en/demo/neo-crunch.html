Crunch: programs and data on the fly

On the Amiga, Imploder and PowerPacker crunched programs. Neo Boing Platinum OS does it as a service of the system: programs and documents take 30 to 50% less space, programs stay ordinary files, and on an old disk they even load faster.

Crunched programs: loading
A program is an ordinary ELF file. The loader unpacks it in 64 KB chunks, only the parts that are needed; the signature stays valid because the content is what gets signed. Figures are estimates from published zstd measurements.
The Disk tile and the Crunch button
The small face shows free space, the large one also what crunching has saved. Crunch re-crunches the bigger folders at the strongest level, in the background and only on mains power. Try switching to battery halfway: the work waits and nothing is lost.
A level per folder, no wasted time
Every folder has a level: no, fast, strong, strongest. Pictures, music and video are already compressed (JPEG, MP3, MP4), so the system recognises them and leaves them alone. Lite uses the fastest level, to spare the battery and a weak processor.
Why not RAR
RAR is only opened (libarchive), because packing into RAR is closed. Packing goes to 7z and ZIP, and RAR's best feature, repairing a damaged archive, comes from an open PAR2 record. Try it: archive, damage, repair.

Neo is a concept. This is a simulation in the browser, not the real system. A nod to Turbo Imploder and PowerPacker; the colour bars are our own drawing. Spec part 7d.
