Cabinet Pro: Magellan, triggers and the Translator

A companion to the Cabinet Pro and Duo demo: what the Cabinet takes from Magellan. A Lister that changes face, a button bank that tears off into a Shelf, actions per filetype, the Mine menu, triggers on events, and the Linux Translator for every command.

Info and preview
Pick a file and press Info, View (F3) or Will it fit? Right-click a file for the actions of its type.
Triggers
Try the other trigger too: move Internet bill.pdf from Home:Letterbox to Home:Bills.
Duo (Opus 4 and Norton Commander)
Source on the left, destination on the right. Tab switches the active panel, a click marks, a double-click opens and Backspace goes back. Clicking the path opens the buffer of recent folders. The right panel is a full USB stick: try copying Pictures and see what “Will it fit?” says.
Button bank and command line
Three pages of buttons. A left click is the main action, a right click the second one (Copy as…, Delete for ever, Crunch strongest), and a middle click or Alt+click shows the Neo DOS command the button runs. Tear the bank off and it becomes a Shelf you can drag around the screen.
Magellan: Lister, filetypes and Mine
The Lister changes face: icons, list, columns or gallery, and “Join into Duo” brings back two panels. Right-click a file for the actions of its type. The Mine menu in the menu bar holds your own commands. The Translator shows the Linux equivalent of every command, or says honestly that there is none.

Neo is a concept. This is a simulation in the browser, not the real system. The ideas come from Directory Opus 4 and 5 (both open source) and Norton Commander; the name, look and drawings are Neo's own.
