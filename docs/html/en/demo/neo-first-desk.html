First Desk: working with documents, not programs

A retro theme in the spirit of the 1983 Lisa, under our own name and with our own drawings: black and white, dotted patterns and mechanical sounds. The principles matter more than the look: tear a sheet off a pad, no Save button, set a document aside, a cartridge carries its documents, and after shutting down the Desk comes back exactly as it was.

Pads instead of “New”
Double-click a pad (Letter, Invoice, Household budget, School essay) to tear off a new sheet with a name and date and open it. Type freely: it saves itself, with no Save button. The little box on the left closes the sheet and it shrinks back into its icon; while it is open, the icon is hollow.
Set aside, eject, switch off
“Set aside” shrinks a document to an icon, but it stays open. “Eject cartridge” saves and neatly closes its documents; put it back and they reopen in the same places. “Good night” switches the computer off, and at start-up everything is as it was.
Tasks as rooms, and Jump
The Tasks menu swaps the whole room: Tax 2027 and Dissertation have their own documents and windows. A finished task is archived as a single case. Jump (or Alt+J): type part of a text and the cursor leaps to it, no mouse needed.

This is a simulation in the browser, not the real system. The real Neo Boing Platinum OS Lite starts from an ISO image; the Neo 500/2000/4000 are concept hardware. Inspired by the principles of the Lisa (1983), the Xerox Star and the Canon Cat. The Lisa name, its fonts and icons belong to others; this is our own bitmap look and our own drawings. Spec part 8b.
