News you choose yourself

Neo News shows only the sources you picked, in the order they arrived. No algorithm pushing stories, no endless scrolling, no tracking. An edition has an end, like a newspaper.

Compulsory news from above

Pick your sources. Turn sources on and off on the left, or add your own RSS. The “Who is this?” button shows who is behind a source and how it is funded.
Compare two sources. The bridge story comes from two sources with different headlines. “Compare” puts them side by side so you can judge. This is a media literacy lesson.
Try the editions. The morning edition has an end: once you have read everything, it says “That's all”. The Teletext view works on an old screen and in CRT mode.

Neo is a concept. This is a simulation in the browser, not the real system. The sources and stories are made-up examples.
