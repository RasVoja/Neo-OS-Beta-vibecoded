Claude help, switched on by you

In Neo, Claude is off until you call it. You choose how far it may go: just a hint, an explanation, or working with you with your approval for every step. You always see what Claude is looking at and what, if anything, leaves the computer.

Change the level. The same question gets a different answer: a hint for learning, an explanation for understanding, and “Work with me” shows the exact command before running it.
Change the profile. For a child, a parent allows Hint at most. During a test the teacher switches help off on every computer in the classroom.
Follow what happens. The menu bar says what Claude sees, below the answer it says what leaves the computer, and the Caretaker logs every change, which can always be undone.

Neo is a concept. This is a simulation in the browser, not the real system. The answers in this demo are pre-written.
