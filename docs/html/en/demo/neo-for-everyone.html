One system, from pocket to workstation

The same NeoOS runs on a pocket device, on a powerful pocket device with an FPGA, on the Neo 500, the Neo 2000 and the Neo 4000 workstation. It serves a child, an adult, a grandmother and a professional just as well. Pick a device and a person and watch the same apps adapt.

What stays the same

  • The same apps, the same file formats and the same settings on every device.
  • A document started on the pocket device continues on the big screen (Flow, over the home network, no cloud).
  • The same child account works everywhere, with the same limits the parent set.
  • Touch, mouse, keyboard, gamepad and voice always work; touch mode only makes targets bigger and adds an on-screen keyboard.
Change the device. On the pocket devices the menu moves to a bottom bar and the icons grow to fit a finger. On the Neo 4000 the same desk spreads over three screens.
Change the person. A child sees five safe apps and their play time, Grandma sees four big buttons and voice, a pro sees the Shell, Cabinet Pro and the meters.
Hand it over. Open Quill, then click “Continue on…”: the same document opens on the other device, right where you stopped.

Neo is a concept. This is a simulation in the browser, not the real system.
