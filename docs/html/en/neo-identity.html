Audio-visual identity · concept, November 2026
NEO

A computer with a soul that stays out of your way.

Everything in one place: the logo, colours, type, materials, Dijana and the residents, sound, motion and the way Neo speaks. The rule is the same for all of it: warm, clear and quiet, with a little humour and a lot of respect for the user's time.

In 50 seconds

Identity in motion

A short film that brings all the elements together: the logo and power-on motif, colours, glass and tiles, Dijana, the three sound themes and the Board. Music and sounds were made by synthesis (CC0).

Logo

Three letters and three stripes

The name NEO in Chakra Petch, an angular typeface reminiscent of the lettering on eighties computer cases. Below it, three stripes: teal for technology, orange for warmth and the text colour for seriousness. The stripes are also the system's signature: they appear on the case, at power-on and at the end of every film.

Clear space

Around the logo, keep free at least the height of the letter E. The stripes are always as wide as the word NEO.

Minimum

The word at 16 px on screen and 6 mm in print. Below that, only the N icon with stripes.

Don't

No shadows, gloss or skewing on the logo, no changing the order of colours in the stripes and no logo over a photograph without a backing.

Colours

Silver and Slate, with two warm sparks

The two system themes share the same colour roles. Silver is light like old office computers, and Slate is dark like stone after rain. Teal is the colour of action, orange the colour of attention, and red is reserved for Dijana's scarf and errors.

Materials

Glass, tiles and shelves

Frosted glass for frames, a solid backing for content. Text never sits directly on glass, and the system increases the blur by itself when the background weakens the contrast.

Characters

Dijana and the residents

Dijana is a basset hound with a red scarf and a small star. She sleeps until you call her, sniffs when you search and never interrupts your work. Residents are other characters who replace her or keep her company, with the same behaviour and their own sounds.

Drawing

Soft shapes, warm browns and creams, the red scarf as the only strong accent. Drawn without outlines, with gentle gradients, to match the glass.

Behaviour

Seven states for every resident: sleeps, wakes up, sniffs, rejoices, mourns, jokes, goes to sleep. At most one joke a day.

Residents

Panda, bear, snake, hedgehog, stork, and licensed characters only with official permission. First candidate for a deal: the snake from “Ide zmija” (“Here Comes the Snake”), dancing to the Turntable.

Sound

The Neo greeting and sound themes

The sound logo is the Neo greeting: five rising tones (C, E, G, C, E) in 240 ms, with a long soft echo. It plays at power-on, at the end of films and in ads. All themes speak the same language: success goes up, an error goes down, a notification is two tones.

Measure

Frequent sounds under 150 ms, rare ones up to 2 s. All themes equally loud, and quieter at night.

Clear space

Sound comes from where the event is on screen, and music dips for a moment.

Music

The Neo theme is warm electronica with an analogue synthesizer, in the spirit of home computers. All synthesized, CC0.

Motion

Fast, soft and always interruptible

Every motion for an action lasts at most 150 ms, while physics (a thrown window, snow) may take longer because it does not hold up work. Motions have a slight bounce at the end, as if things had weight. “Less motion” turns it all off.

Motion curve · try it
cubic-bezier(.2, .9, .25, 1.15)
Rules
  • Action: 120 to 150 ms, interruptible at once.
  • Appearing: from below and from the direction the thing comes from.
  • Physics: Newton, not the main processor.
  • On battery: one level of beauty less.
Voice

How Neo speaks

Like a patient neighbour who knows computers: short, plain, with a solution. Everyday words in Easy mode, technical terms in Pro. Never the user's fault, never a sales pitch.

Tone

Warm and short. Informal “you” in the system, except in Pro mode and business profiles.

Humour

Rarely, and only when nothing is on fire. Never in an error that costs data.
